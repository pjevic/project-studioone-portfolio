# Project StudioOne – Product Management Portfolio

This repository contains a complete product lifecycle case study for **Project StudioOne** – a premium live casino infrastructure product.

---

## 📁 Documents

- [Product Concept Document](./01_Product_Concept_Document.pdf) – market problem, financials, roadmap, competitive analysis
- [Product Vision Document](./02_Product_Vision_Document.pdf) – elevator speech, value proposition, differentiation
- [User Stories](./03_User_Story_Document.pdf) – PM, Operator, and Player perspectives with acceptance criteria
- [Beta Plan](./04_Beta_Plan_Document.pdf) – testing strategy, KPIs, logistics, legal considerations
- [Demand Generation Plan](./05_Demand_Generation_Plan.pdf) – marketing objectives, campaigns, metrics, influencers

---

## 🎯 What This Portfolio Demonstrates

- Defining market problems and opportunities
- Structuring a product concept with financials and roadmap
- Translating vision into user stories
- Planning beta testing and validation
- Creating a demand generation strategy

---

## 🧠 Context

This portfolio was developed as part of the **IBM Product Manager Professional Certificate** program. It reflects a real-world approach to product management — from concept to launch — applied to a live casino infrastructure product.
